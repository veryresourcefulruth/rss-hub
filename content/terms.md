# Terms of Engagement for Agency Workers

These terms set out how Resourceful Staffing Support (RSS) finds you temporary work in nurseries, and what you and RSS each agree to. Read them with your Key Information Document, which RSS gives you before you agree to these terms.

## 1. Who we are and your status

**1.1** "RSS", "we" and "us" means Resourceful Ruth Solutions Ltd, trading as Resourceful Staffing Support, of Unit 2B Spence Mills, Mill Lane, Leeds LS13 3HE. "You" means the agency worker who signs these terms. A "Client" or "setting" is a nursery or other early years provider that asks RSS for staff. An "Assignment" is a period of work you do for a Client through RSS.

**1.2** RSS acts as an **employment business** under the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003. That means RSS engages you, pays you and finds you temporary work with Clients. You work under the Client's supervision and direction while on an Assignment, but you are not employed by the Client.

**1.3** These terms are a **contract for services**. You are a *worker*, not an employee of RSS or of any Client. This contract does not make you an employee, even if you work for RSS regularly or over a long period.

**1.4** RSS pays you through PAYE. RSS deducts income tax and National Insurance from your pay and pays employer's National Insurance.

**1.5** Neither side has to offer or accept a set amount of work. RSS does not promise you any minimum number of hours. You can turn down any Assignment you are offered, and turning one down will not count against you.

**1.6** Before you agreed to these terms, RSS gave you a **Key Information Document** summarising your pay, deductions and who pays you. If anything in it changes, RSS will give you an updated copy within one week of the change, or before you next start work if that is sooner.

## 2. The work we find for you and how assignments are offered

**2.1** The work RSS finds for you is temporary cover as an early years practitioner or nursery assistant in registered early years settings in England. This includes caring for babies and young children, supporting learning and play under the EYFS, helping with meals, nappy changing, sleep and outdoor play, and any other reasonable duties the setting gives you that fit your training and experience.

**2.2** Assignments are offered and managed through the **RSS Hub**. You can see open shifts, accept or decline shifts assigned to you, record your hours and see your checks there. RSS may also contact you by phone, text or email.

**2.3** When RSS offers you an Assignment, it will tell you:

- the setting's name and address;
- the start date, times and expected length (if known);
- the type of work and any particular requirements (for example Level 3 or Paediatric First Aid);
- your hourly rate of pay;
- any known health and safety risks and what the setting does about them; and
- any experience, training or qualifications the setting requires.

If RSS gives you any of this information verbally, it will confirm it in writing (for example in the RSS Hub) within three business days.

**2.4** An Assignment is only confirmed once you have accepted it in the RSS Hub (or confirmed it in writing to RSS) and RSS has confirmed the booking.

## 3. What you must have in place before you can be booked

**3.1** RSS will not book you onto any shift until all of the following are in place and checked by RSS. The RSS Hub will show you what is still outstanding.

**Vetting**

- An **Enhanced DBS certificate with a check of the children's barred list** (Child Workforce). RSS records the certificate number, issue date, DBS type and, if you subscribe, your DBS Update Service number.
- Proof of your **right to work** in the UK. Where you hold a time-limited permission (for example a Student visa), RSS will record when it expires and any limit on your hours.
- Photographic **ID**.
- **Two references**, at least one from your most recent employer or course tutor.
- These **Terms of Engagement**, signed.

**Core training** (each valid for one year from the date it was issued)

- Safeguarding Level 2
- The Prevent Duty and British Values
- Food Safety & Hygiene
- Health & Safety in the Workplace
- Safe Sleep
- Blossoming Babies

**RSS induction**

- **RSS Childcare 101**, passed in full, including the declaration at the end.

**3.2** Some shifts also need a **Level 3 early years qualification** or a current **Paediatric First Aid** certificate. You will only be offered those shifts if RSS holds proof of them.

**3.3** It is your responsibility to renew your training before it expires and upload the new certificate to the RSS Hub. If any check or course expires, you cannot be booked until it is renewed, and RSS may need to cancel shifts you have already accepted.

**3.4** **Disclosure.** You must tell RSS straight away, and before your next shift, if:

- you are arrested, charged, cautioned, convicted or reprimanded for any offence;
- you are subject to any order or investigation that could affect your suitability to work with children;
- anything changes that could affect your DBS certificate or barred-list status; or
- your right to work in the UK changes or is about to end.

Under the EYFS, settings must make sure staff are suitable, so RSS will share relevant information with Clients and with Ofsted or the local authority designated officer (LADO) where the law or safeguarding requires it. Failing to disclose may lead to these terms being ended immediately.

## 4. Accepting, declining, cancelling and ending assignments

**4.1** You may accept or decline any shift offered to you. Please respond in the RSS Hub as soon as you can so the setting can plan.

**4.2** **If you need to cancel** a shift you have accepted, tell RSS as early as possible and at least **24 hours** before the start, by phone and in the RSS Hub. Do not contact the setting instead of RSS. Repeated late cancellations or not turning up without good reason may mean RSS stops offering you work.

**4.3** **If the Client cancels** a shift, RSS will tell you as soon as it knows. If the cancellation happens after you have arrived, RSS will pay you for the hours you have worked.

**4.4** **Ending an Assignment.** You or RSS may end an Assignment at any time by giving **24 hours'** notice. RSS or the Client may end an Assignment immediately and without notice where:

- there is a safeguarding concern;
- you are not suitable for the work or your vetting or training lapses;
- you have seriously breached these terms or the setting's policies; or
- the Client no longer needs cover.

**4.5** If an Assignment ends early, RSS will still pay you for all hours you have worked.

## 5. Pay, timesheets and deductions

**5.1** **Rate of pay.** RSS will tell you your hourly rate for each Assignment before you accept it. Your standard rates are set out in Schedule 1. Your pay will never be less than the National Minimum Wage or National Living Wage for your age. From 1 April 2026 the National Living Wage (age 21 and over) is £12.71 an hour, and the rate for ages 18 to 20 is £10.85 an hour.

**5.2** **Timesheets.** At the end of each shift, record your actual start time, finish time and unpaid breaks in the RSS Hub. The setting's manager approves or queries your hours there. If hours are queried, RSS will look into it and tell you the outcome before the next pay date if it can.

**5.3** **When you are paid.** RSS pays you **monthly** in arrears by bank transfer, **on or before the 10th of the month**, for all approved hours worked up to the **27th of the previous month**. You will receive a payslip for each payment.

**5.4** **RSS pays you whatever the Client does.** RSS will pay you for all the hours you work on an Assignment, even if the Client does not pay RSS, pays late, or disputes the invoice. RSS will not hold back your pay because you have not got a timesheet signed by the Client, but RSS may need to check your hours with the Client first.

**5.5** **Deductions.** RSS will deduct income tax, employee National Insurance and, if you are enrolled, pension contributions, and any other deduction the law requires (for example student loan repayments or an attachment of earnings order). RSS will not make any other deduction from your pay unless you have agreed to it in writing beforehand.

**5.6** **No fees.** RSS will never charge you a fee for finding you work, for registering, for training it requires, or for using the RSS Hub.

**5.7** **Overpayments.** If RSS pays you too much by mistake, it will tell you and agree with you how the overpayment is paid back.

## 6. Holidays, sick pay and pension

**Holiday**

**6.1** You are entitled to paid holiday of 5.6 weeks a year, worked out in proportion to the hours you actually work. The holiday year runs from **1 January to 31 December**.

**6.2** Because your hours are irregular, RSS pays your holiday as **rolled-up holiday pay**: an extra **12.07%** of your pay for the hours you work, added to each payment. Your payslip shows this amount as a separate line. It is the payment for your holiday, so when you take time off you will not be paid again for it. Please plan to set this money aside for your time off.

**6.3** You choose when to take time off, simply by not accepting shifts. If you have accepted a shift and then want that day off, follow the cancellation rules in section 4.

**6.4** RSS keeps records of your holiday and holiday pay for six years, as the law requires.

**Sick pay**

**6.5** If you are ill and cannot work a shift you have accepted, tell RSS by phone as early as possible, and before the shift starts. Do not just message the setting.

**6.6** You may be entitled to **Statutory Sick Pay (SSP)**. From 6 April 2026, SSP is paid from the first day of sickness absence and there is no lower earnings limit. The weekly rate is the lower of £123.25 or 80% of your normal average weekly earnings. SSP is only paid for days on which you would otherwise have worked (for example a shift you had accepted). RSS may ask for a self-certificate for absences of up to seven days and a fit note after that.

**6.7** If you have had vomiting or diarrhoea, or have an infectious illness, you must not attend a setting until the time the setting's health policy requires has passed (usually 48 hours after symptoms stop).

**Pension**

**6.8** If you meet the age and earnings conditions set by the government, RSS will automatically enrol you into a workplace pension and pay employer contributions. You can opt out. If you do not meet the conditions, you can still ask to join. RSS will write to you with the details.

## 7. Equal treatment after 12 weeks

**7.1** **From day one** of any Assignment you have the right to use the setting's shared facilities (such as the staff room, kitchen, toilets and parking, if staff have them) on the same terms as its own staff, and to be told about any job vacancies at that setting.

**7.2** **After 12 calendar weeks** in the same role with the same Client, the Agency Workers Regulations 2010 give you the right to the same basic working and employment conditions as if you had been hired directly by that Client, including pay and holiday. The weeks do not have to be consecutive: some breaks pause the count rather than restart it.

**7.3** RSS will tell you if you are approaching 12 weeks with a Client and will check what the Client's own staff in a comparable role receive. If you think you are not being treated equally, tell RSS in writing and RSS will reply in writing within 28 days.

## 8. Working hours, rest breaks and student visa limits

**8.1** Under the Working Time Regulations 1998 you should not work more than an average of 48 hours a week unless you choose to opt out in writing. You can cancel an opt-out by giving RSS seven days' written notice.

**8.2** You are entitled to a rest break of at least 20 minutes if you work more than six hours in a day, at least 11 hours' rest between working days, and at least 24 hours off each week. Breaks at a setting are normally unpaid unless the Assignment says otherwise.

**8.3** **Student visa holders.** If you hold a Student visa, your visa sets a limit on the hours you may work, usually **20 hours a week during term time** for degree-level study, with full-time work allowed only during your university's official vacations. This limit covers **all** paid work you do, not just work through RSS. You agree to:

- give RSS your university's official term and vacation dates each year;
- tell RSS about any other paid work you do; and
- never accept shifts that would take you over your limit.

**8.4** The RSS Hub will stop you being booked over your recorded weekly limit for RSS work. Working over your visa limit can lead to your visa being cancelled, and RSS must stop offering you work if it believes you are breaching your visa conditions.

## 9. Conduct, safeguarding and confidentiality at the setting

**At every setting you will:**

- arrive on time, ready to work, and sign in and out using the setting's system;
- wear plain, practical clothing and closed shoes, and any ID badge RSS or the setting gives you;
- follow the setting's policies and procedures, including safeguarding, health and safety, behaviour, fire and intimate care, and the reasonable instructions of the manager or room leader;
- work within the EYFS 2026 and never be left as the only adult responsible for children unless the setting has confirmed you meet its requirements for that role; and
- treat children, families and staff with kindness and respect.

**You must not:**

- use your **personal mobile phone, smartwatch or camera** in rooms where children are present. Store them where the setting tells you;
- take photos or videos of children, or post anything about a setting, its children, families or staff on **social media**;
- offer **private babysitting** or any other paid childcare to a setting's families, or give them your personal contact details;
- attend a setting under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or smoke or vape on the premises; or
- discuss children or families outside the setting.

**Safeguarding**

**9.1** The welfare of the child comes first. If you are worried about a child's safety or wellbeing, or about the behaviour of any adult (including another member of staff or RSS staff), report it **straight away** to the setting's Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) or deputy and then tell RSS. If it is about the DSL or the manager, contact RSS directly or the local authority designated officer (LADO). If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.

**9.2** Never promise a child that you will keep a secret. Write down what you saw or heard, in the words used, and sign and date it.

**9.3** You can raise concerns about wrongdoing (whistleblowing) to RSS, the setting or Ofsted without being treated unfairly for doing so.

**Confidentiality**

**9.4** Keep confidential everything you learn about children, families, staff and the setting's business, both during and after an Assignment. This duty continues after these terms end.

**Being offered a job by a setting**

**9.5** If a setting offers you a permanent or direct job, please tell RSS. You are free to accept it. RSS will not charge you anything or penalise you. Any fee is a matter between RSS and the setting.

## 10. Your data, DBS Update Service checks and what nurseries see

**10.1** RSS is the data controller for the personal information it holds about you and handles it under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. RSS uses it to check you are suitable and allowed to work, find you work, pay you and meet its legal duties.

**10.2** **What settings see.** When RSS books you (or proposes to book you) into a setting, the setting's manager can see your **Agency Staff Profile** in the RSS Hub. This shows your name, photo, qualifications, training and expiry dates, your DBS certificate number, issue date and type, whether you are on the DBS Update Service, and confirmation that RSS has checked your right to work. Settings need this to meet their own safeguarding duties under the EYFS. Settings do not see your bank details, home address, ID documents or references.

**10.3** **DBS Update Service.** If you subscribe to the DBS Update Service, you agree that RSS may carry out online status checks on your certificate while you are registered with RSS, and you will keep your subscription active.

**10.4** RSS keeps copies of your documents securely in the RSS Hub and only for as long as it needs them, including the periods the law requires (for example six years for pay and holiday records). You can ask to see the information RSS holds about you at any time. RSS's privacy notice gives full details of your rights.

## 11. Raising concerns and complaints

**11.1** If you have a problem with an Assignment, a setting, your pay or anything else, speak to your RSS coordinator first. If it is not sorted out, put your complaint in writing to Ruth at RSS, who will reply in writing within **14 days**.

**11.2** Settings may also raise concerns about you with RSS. RSS will tell you what the concern is, give you the chance to respond, and tell you the outcome, except where safeguarding rules mean information cannot be shared yet.

**11.3** If you are hurt or involved in an accident or near miss at a setting, report it to the setting so it can be recorded in its accident book, and tell RSS the same day.

**11.4** You can get free, impartial advice about your rights from Acas. The **Fair Work Agency**, which began work in April 2026, enforces agency workers' rights including minimum wage, holiday pay and the rules on employment businesses.

## 12. Changing or ending these terms, and general terms

**12.1** **Changes.** RSS may update these terms, for example when the law changes. RSS will tell you in writing (including through the RSS Hub) before any change takes effect, and will not change your pay rate for an Assignment you have already accepted.

**12.2** **Ending these terms.** You or RSS may end these terms at any time by telling the other in writing. RSS may end them immediately if you seriously breach them, give false information, fail to disclose something under section 3.4, or are no longer suitable or allowed to work with children. RSS will still pay you for all hours worked and any holiday pay owed.

**12.3** Sections 5.4, 9.4 and 10 continue after these terms end.

**12.4** These terms, your Key Information Document and the details of each Assignment make up the whole agreement between you and RSS. If any part of these terms cannot be enforced, the rest still applies.

**12.5** These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts and employment tribunals of England and Wales deal with any dispute.
