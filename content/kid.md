# Key Information Document

**Resourceful Staffing Support (RSS)**: agency workers paid through PAYE by RSS

This document gives you the key facts about who will pay you, how much, and what will be taken off your pay, **before** you agree terms with us. Read it alongside the RSS Terms of Engagement. If any of these details change, we will give you an updated copy within one week of the change, or before you next start work if that is sooner.

**If you have a concern about your agency**

- **Fair Work Agency (FWA)** enforces agency workers' rights. Phone 0345 161 6000 (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm), email contact@fairworkagency.gov.uk, or use the online "pay and work rights complaint" form on GOV.UK.
- **Acas** gives free, confidential advice on 0300 123 1100 or at acas.org.uk.

## Pay, deductions and holiday

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rate of pay** | £13.00 per hour as standard. The exact rate for each shift is confirmed before you accept it, and is never below the National Minimum Wage or National Living Wage for your age |
| **Pay intervals** | Monthly in arrears, paid on or before the 10th of the month for approved hours worked up to the 27th of the previous month, by bank transfer |
| **Deductions required by law** | Income Tax and employee National Insurance (through PAYE); workplace pension contributions if you are auto-enrolled; student or postgraduate loan repayments if HMRC tells us to; any court or government order |
| **Other deductions** | None |
| **Fees for goods or services** | None. RSS does not charge you for registering, finding you work, training it requires, or using the RSS Hub |
| **Other benefits** | Access to the RSS Hub and RSS Childcare 101 induction training, free of charge |
| **Holiday** | 5.6 weeks a year, in proportion to the hours you work (holiday year 1 January to 31 December). Paid as rolled-up holiday pay: an extra 12.07% of your pay for hours worked, shown separately on each payslip. You are not paid again when you take time off |

## Other information

- RSS will pay you for all hours you work, even if the nursery does not pay RSS.
- You or RSS may end a shift booking with 24 hours' notice (see the Terms of Engagement, section 4).
- You are entitled to Statutory Sick Pay from the first day of sickness absence, if you qualify.
